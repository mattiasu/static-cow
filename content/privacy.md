## Privacy

No tracking. No analytics. The only data I collect is your email address if you choose to sign up for the newsletter. I use it solely to send you new posts — nothing else, ever.

Emails are sent via [Resend](https://resend.com), a transactional email service. Your address is stored in a database I control and is not shared with anyone.

The only thing I store in a cookie is whether someone on your computer has signed up for the newsletter — not who you are, just that someone did. I guess you understand. 😁

If you want your email removed, just reach out at [mattias@uhlegard.se](mailto:mattias@uhlegard.se) and I'll delete it promptly.

If you email me directly, I'll read it and reply. That's the extent of it.
